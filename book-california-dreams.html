California Dreams

The Art of Stanley Mouse • By Stanley Mouse Miller • Art monograph • Soft Skull Press hardcover • 224 pages • Published June 2015 • ISBN 9781593765460

The first comprehensive collection of work by Stanley Mouse, one of the pioneering artists of psychedelic poster art in the late 1960s. Across more than five decades it gathers posters, album covers, T-shirt designs and later fine-art paintings: about 240 pieces, many unpublished before, including early versions of his best-known images.

Mouse is best known for the posters and album covers he made, often with Alton Kelley, during San Francisco's counterculture years and into the 1970s, work that still stands for the color and experimental spirit of that time. The book also reaches back to his Detroit hot-rod years and forward into landscapes and figurative painting.

We already carried this on our shelves. After Mouse Studios reached out on Instagram, we gave it its own page and pinned it on the Literary Atlas at the Haight-Ashbury house where Mouse and Kelley lived and made posters in 1966–67. Soft Skull Press published it in 2015; the text is by Blair Jackson.
