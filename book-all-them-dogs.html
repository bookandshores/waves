All Them Dogs

Djamel White • Novel • Riverhead, 2026 • Longlisted for the 2026 Booker Prize

Tony Ward comes back to West Dublin after five years hiding in England and goes to work for Flute Walsh, the enforcer for a local crime boss. Their partnership turns into a secret, dangerous attraction while Tony's past closes in. Djamel White's debut is savage, erotic, and unexpectedly tender.

A gangland thriller with a beating heart. The voice alone is worth the ride.
