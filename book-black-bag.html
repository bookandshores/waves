Black Bag

Luke Kennard • Novel • 2026 • Shortlisted for the 2026 Booker Prize

A broke, out-of-work actor takes a strange gig: sitting at the back of university lectures for a whole autumn term, zipped inside an oversized black bag, for a professor's psychology experiment. Soon a professor of post-humanism has research questions of her own, starting with whether you can love someone hidden in a bag. Meanwhile his childhood friend and flatmate sees a way to make money out of it. Kennard got the idea from a real 1960s black bag experiment.

The weirdest premise on the shortlist, and the funniest.

The Booker judges said: “This of-the-moment satire considers anonymity and disgust, online rackets, and what it means to find love in our unstable times. We admired its sharply crafted sentences and humour.”
