Paradiso 17

Hannah Lillith Assadi • Literary fiction • Knopf hardcover • 320 pages • March 17, 2026 • 2026 National Book Award finalist, Fiction

Buy Now

Born in one desert and dying in another, Sufien feels his spirit slipping finally to the next world, which has beckoned him since childhood. But once more, he returns in his mind to the beginning of it all: Palestine. Raised in Safad on the precipice of the 1948 Nakba, Sufien is forced to leave the only home he’s ever known, the one on the hill with a beautiful blue door. This is the precise moment when time stops making sense. He spends the rest of his life propelled forward, always on his way—although to what or where, he is never quite sure.

In the dusty, oil-rich desert of Kuwait, he meets his first love and decides he must leave his family. Years later, studying in a small Italian university town, he spends his youth wrapped up in the sweet promise of the West and the forgetful assurance of wine. When life takes him to a gritty New York, he discovers his true vocation and falls for a Jewish woman born into a wholly different world. Finally, he finds himself recalled to the wild, vast open skies of the desert in Arizona.

The lyrical pages of Paradiso 17 weave in and out of time and space, beginning at the end and ending at the beginning. Like all of our dead, Sufien still speaks, the book begins. Listen: This is his story.

Description from Penguin Random House. Finalist status from the National Book Foundation.

Assadi is a National Book Foundation 5 Under 35 honoree, and Paradiso 17 was also longlisted for the Women’s Prize. It is a 2026 National Book Award finalist for Fiction. Winners are announced November 18.
