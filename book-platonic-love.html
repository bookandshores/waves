Platonic Love

Mieko Kanai, translated by Polly Barton

Short stories by Mieko Kanai, translated by Polly Barton • New Directions paperback • 160 pages • Published 27 April 2027 • ISBN 9780811239424 • Forthcoming

New Directions calls this a shocking collection of absurdist, strangely moving, and uncanny short stories, gleefully taking familiar tropes to horrifying conclusions. The stories, first published in Japan in the 1970s, include a cat who looks like Orson Welles and is the grim reaper; a murder in a secret society of chivalric knights in a high school; and an utterly disturbing tale involving rabbits and fathers.

Polly Barton translated the collection from the Japanese. New Directions places the book in a line from Tanizaki and Kobo Abe to Haruki Murakami, Hiroko Oyamada, and Sayaka Murata.

A title in our Japanese Lit collection. This is a collection of short stories, and New Directions does not name a single place, so the book is not pinned on the B&S Literary Atlas. Go There. Read It.
