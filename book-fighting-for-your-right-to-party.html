A Foreign Spin on 100 Korean Music Gems · Jung Wooyoung

A Foreign Spin on 100 Korean Music Gems • By Jung Wooyoung • Poets & Punks • 2026 • Out now

Jung Wooyoung's book looks at Korean popular music through 100 albums and songs spanning 1967–2022, and through the perspectives of ten international collectors, DJs and writers. Poets & Punks, the publisher, is based in London and Seoul.
