Fifteen books in Fiction, Nonfiction and Translated Literature, five per category, and the winners are named November 18. Seven independent publishers are in the running across the five categories, so the small guys are swinging. A sentence each, then go pick your horse.

Quick Answers

When are the 2026 National Book Award winners announced?

Wednesday, November 18, 2026, at the 77th National Book Awards Ceremony & Benefit Dinner, livestreamed on the National Book Foundation's website and YouTube. The finalists read the night before, November 17, at NYU Skirball.

What do the winners get?

$10,000, a bronze medal and a statue. Each finalist gets $1,000 and a bronze medal. In Translated Literature the prize is split evenly between author and translator.

How many books were submitted?

1,912 across five categories: 451 in Fiction, 727 in Nonfiction, 285 in Poetry, 159 in Translated Literature, and 290 in Young People's Literature.

What about Poetry and Young People's Literature?

Those finalists are on the National Book Foundation's announcement. Here we stuck to our shelves: fiction, nonfiction and translation. Our four Weekly Reads this week all come from this list, and the Booker shortlist is still waiting on November 9.
