Mondo Tokyo

Dispatches from a Secret Japan · Patrick Macias

Dispatches from a Secret Japan • By Patrick Macias • Sutherland House • 2024 • ISBN 9781990823299 • Out now

Patrick Macias writes from early-2000s Tokyo and the people who came looking for a stranger life there. Sutherland House's book page calls Tokyo in the early aughts "a weird place," and points to the "wide-eyed ex-pats, weirdoes, and dreamers who came to Tokyo."

A Weekly Reads pick for November 15, and a stop on Seoul to Tokyo on the B&S Literary Atlas. The pin is a dashed ring at the Tokyo stop, marking the city. Go There. Read It.
