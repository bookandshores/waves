Cursed Thing

Nashi, translated by Jim Rion

A novel by Nashi, translated by Jim Rion • Octopus paperback • Published 25 February 2027 • ISBN 9781846019180 • Forthcoming

Octopus Publishing Group presents forum posts, creepypastas, mysterious email chains, tampered audio, and, littered across the city, distorted images of a young woman's face. When the narrator begins to research, the materials seem unconnected; then coincidences turn into patterns, and everything is linked.

The publisher calls Nashi a leading writer in Japan's “fake documentary” horror boom, and notes that Jim Rion also translated Uketsu's Strange Pictures series.

A title in our Japanese Lit collection. Octopus describes images of a young woman's face littered across the city, and does not name the city, so the book is not pinned on the B&S Literary Atlas. Go There. Read It.
