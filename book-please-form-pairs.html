Please Form Pairs

Chiren Kina, translated by Haydn Trowell

A novel by Chiren Kina, translated by Haydn Trowell • Octopus paperback • Published 27 August 2026 • ISBN 9781846017537 • Out now

Octopus Publishing Group presents the #1 Japanese hit as a candy-coated death game with three rules: please form pairs; you cannot pair with someone twice; anyone without a partner will be executed. Mishin Mizushima isn't nearly cool enough by Japanese standards. Even her mum has ditched her for a new lover, leaving her alone in their tiny apartment.

At school, the publisher writes, towering alpha females move and think as a single, terrifyingly beautiful organism. Then a new teacher questions the natural order with a simple instruction and unleashes a gladiatorial catfight for survival. Haydn Trowell translated the book from the Japanese.

A title in our Japanese Lit collection. Octopus places the story at a school in Japan and does not name a city, so the book is not pinned on the B&S Literary Atlas. Go There. Read It.
