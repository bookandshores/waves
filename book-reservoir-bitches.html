Reservoir Bitches

Dahlia de la Cerda, translated by Julia Sanches and Heather Cleary

Stories by Dahlia de la Cerda, translated by Julia Sanches and Heather Cleary • Feminist Press paperback • 192 pages • Published 10 September 2024 • ISBN 9781558613119

Thirteen linked stories about Mexican women who fight, cheat, cry, kill and lie their way to survival, from the daughter of a cartel boss to a houseful of spinster seamstresses.

Longlisted for the 2025 International Booker Prize. Translated from the Spanish by Julia Sanches and Heather Cleary.
