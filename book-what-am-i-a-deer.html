What Am I, A Deer?

Polly Barton • Fiction • Fitzcarraldo Editions paperback • Out now

Buy Now

A young woman with little interest in games takes a job at a famous gaming company in Frankfurt, set on reinventing herself. Then a nice-eyed stranger returns her forgotten umbrella, and a year-long whirlwind of obsession follows, along with feverish karaoke trips that echo with Whitney Houston and The Cure. It's the debut novel from Polly Barton, the translator of Butter, and it's on the 2026 Goldsmiths Prize shortlist.

Sources: Fitzcarraldo Editions; Goldsmiths Prize 2026 shortlist.

For anyone who has ever meant every word of a power ballad in a dark little karaoke box.
