Jazz Kissa

Japan's Greatest Listening Cafes · Katsumasa Kusunose

Photography by Katsumasa Kusunose • Foreword by Devon Turnbull (Ojas) • ERG Media • ISBN 9789083532004 • €120 • Out now

From the heart of Tokyo to the most remote corners of the country, listening bars known as Jazz Kissa have shaped Japanese musical culture since the 1940s. Photographer and cultural historian Katsumasa Kusunose spent more than fifteen years documenting these cafés and the people who keep them going. This is his work as a large-format photography book, with a traditional obi band and a cover inspired by the texture of Altec A7 speakers.

The book includes a foreword by Devon Turnbull, founder of Ojas, and a look at hi-fi equipment design, from Altec A7s to JBL speakers and bespoke amplifiers. ERG Media printed it on specially sourced Japanese paper that echoes the tactile texture of the Altec A7 speaker finish.

A Weekly Reads pick for November 15, and a stop on Seoul to Tokyo on the B&S Literary Atlas. The pin sits at the Tokyo stop. Go There. Read It.
