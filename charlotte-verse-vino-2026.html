Before the Night

Got a ticket or not, here's the plan.

Why it matters

Verse & Vino, presented by PNC, is the Library Foundation's signature annual fundraiser. For more than a decade, it's been a night of readers and bestselling authors in support of the Library.

Read these first

  • Want a mystery? Foley's Miss Marple, written with the Christie estate.
  • Want a road trip? Fitzgerald's walk along Johnny Appleseed's trail.
  • Want a hunt? Mosley's King Oliver, coast to coast.
  • Want to be moved? Kuang's language-immersion summer.

No ticket?

Same four books, read on your couch. Pick one, or give the whole stack. Not sure which? A Bookshop.org gift card lets them choose.

Quick Answers

Who are the Verse & Vino 2026 authors?

Isaac Fitzgerald (American Rambler), Lucy Foley (Murder at the Grand Alpine Hotel), R. F. Kuang (Taipei Story), and Walter Mosley (Hanging and Burning).

When and where is Verse & Vino 2026?

Thursday, November 12, 2026, 6:00 to 9:30 pm at the Charlotte Convention Center.

Can I still get tickets?

No. The Library Foundation lists the event as sold out. You can still read all four.

Looking for something for the kids?

EpicFest is the Library's free children's book festival at ImaginOn on Saturday, November 7. See our EpicFest 2026 reading list, or browse the whole bookstore.
