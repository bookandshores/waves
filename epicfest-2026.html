Quick Answers

Who are the EpicFest 2026 authors?

Ashley Belote, Ben Clanton, Brittany J. Thurman, Kimberly Wilson, T. Z. Layton, Phuc Tran, Susie Yi, and Winsome Bingham.

When and where is EpicFest 2026?

Saturday, November 7, 2026, at ImaginOn in Charlotte. It opens at 10 am, and the all-day activities run until 3 pm.

Is EpicFest free?

Yes. The Library describes it as a free literary festival for children and families.
