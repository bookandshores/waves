EpicFest 2026

A free day of books for kids and families at ImaginOn, now in its 10th year. Eight authors and illustrators are coming. Here's one book from each, so the kids know who they're meeting.

When
Saturday, November 7, 2026. Opens at 10 am, with activities until 3 pm.
Where
ImaginOn, 300 E. 7th Street, Charlotte
Cost
Free
Also
An If You Give a Mouse a Cookie performance at noon and Lucky Diaz at 3 pm, both in the McColl Theater. Full schedule at cmlibrary.org/epicfest.

Quick Answers

Who are the EpicFest 2026 authors?

Ashley Belote, Ben Clanton, Brittany J. Thurman, Kimberly Wilson, T. Z. Layton, Phuc Tran, Susie Yi, and Winsome Bingham.

When and where is EpicFest 2026?

Saturday, November 7, 2026, at ImaginOn in Charlotte. It opens at 10 am, and the all-day activities run until 3 pm.

Is EpicFest free?

Yes. The Library describes it as a free literary festival for children and families.
